# Matching Scarves

Entry for make.fun cutest-picture-01 ("Make the cutest picture").

A cream kitten with big blue eyes and a mint knitted scarf hugs a sleeping
day-old duckling that wears a tiny matching mint scarf of its own, on a
picnic blanket at golden hour — a daisy tucked behind the kitten's ear
and a basket of daisies beside them.

`main.py` validates the bundled artwork (JPEG structure, dimensions,
competition gate) and publishes it as `/output/entry.png`.
